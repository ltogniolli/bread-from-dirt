The world changes quietly at first

Everything became copyable.
Except trust.

Fifteen years after leaving the man who chose an impossible machine over their life together, Bia sees a chipped spoon in a viral video and knows who it was that just changed the world.

The plans are anonymous, open, and impossible to contain. From her dispatch desk in Osasco, Bia watches the routes empty while São Paulo learns what abundance costs: livelihoods collapse, governments and companies try to regain control, and finding what you need becomes easy. Knowing whom to trust does not.

About the author

Leonel Togniolli

Leonel Togniolli is a technology executive with more than 26 years of experience who once spent far too long wondering how to explain the arrival of post-scarcity without talking about AI. The result is Bread from Dirt.
